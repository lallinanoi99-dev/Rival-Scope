Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Analysis and PDF export are free — there are no AI costs, and the page reader has a fair-use limit of 40 pages per day. Stamping a report on BOT Chain costs a tiny network fee in BOT, paid from your own wallet.

Which sites can it read?

Any public web page that loads without a login. We read the one page you paste — usually the homepage — not the whole site. If a page is behind bot protection or built entirely in JavaScript, you can paste its text instead.

Is this legal / fair?

RivalScope only reads public pages, the same way a visitor would: one page per site, no logins, no cookies, no scraping of private data. Reports describe what a page says on a given date — they're not statements of fact about any company.

Does it make things up?

No. RivalScope doesn't use AI — it reads the page and applies clear, rule-based checks. Every profile field comes with a quote copied from the page; if something isn't on the page, it's shown as “not shown” instead of a guess. Headline ideas are templates matched to your gaps, so only use the ones that are true for you.

What goes on BOT Chain?

Only a fingerprint (a hash) of the report, the domains, the number of sites, the date and a link to your previous report. The report itself stays in your browser — the proof link shows the domains, date and fingerprint.

Do I need a wallet?

No. You only need MetaMask to stamp a report. Analysis, the full report and PDF export work without one.
